How It Works

🧠 V1 — Local Signals
Detects homoglyphs, digit substitution, brand impersonation, suspicious TLDs, Reply-To mismatch, and phishing subject keywords.
🤖 AI Scoring
Signals are sent to a local Ollama model (llama3.2) which provides a natural language analysis and a risk score.
🌐 V2 — Network Intel
Adds real-time checks: WHOIS domain age, MX record presence, SPF and DMARC record validation.
🔒 Privacy-First
All AI analysis runs locally via Ollama. No email data is sent to external cloud services.
